# Mechanical Clan Field Aptitude — Alpha Slice 133

The Corrected Third Printing defines Field Aptitude as a Phenotype-linked
sub-Trait for trueborn Clan characters (printed pp. 121–122). It is not a
purchasable Trait and does not award XP. It applies a permanent −1 TN modifier
to Skills in the character’s appropriate training Field.

The governed phenotype associations are:

- Aerospace → Clan Aerospace Warrior / Clan Fighter Pilot
- Elemental → Clan Elemental
- MechWarrior → Clan MechWarrior
- Normal Human → no Clan Field Aptitude

The existing phenotype catalog now preserves the canonical aptitude identity,
associated Field identity, and −1 TN effect metadata. Validation requires a
Clan identity, trueborn origin, matching Phenotype, and the exact source-defined
Field association. Freeborn characters may use Normal Human and do not require
a Phenotype.

Phenotype Attribute modifiers, maximums, and Exceptional Attribute interaction
remain governed by the existing Slice 120 legality engine. Clan Fields are
still reference-only; Clan creation, Clan Field purchasing, Clan Life Modules,
and Clan readiness remain disabled.

Source: *A Time of War — Corrected Third Printing*, pp. 109 and 121–122;
applicable v4.0 errata reviewed.

# Alpha Slice 165-B4 — governed Skill and Trait destinations

This slice adds a canonical resolver for the Homeworld Clan destination strings recovered in Slice 165-A. Concrete Skills, Clan Protocol subjects, fixed Traits, and the source-defined Fire Mandrill secondary-language set resolve to canonical family IDs with source provenance.

Open domains such as Interest/Theology/Any, Survival/Any, Tactics/Any, and Natural Aptitude/Any are represented as governed domains rather than literal `/Any` Skills. They remain unresolved until a complete source-legal subject catalog is available. Invalid destinations fail safely.

The resolver is connected to B1 choice validation, but no specific-Clan awards or transactions are applied. Clan readiness, public import, and player-facing selection remain disabled. B5/B6 work remains responsible for complete open-domain catalogs and transaction-time legality.

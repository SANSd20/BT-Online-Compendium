Career, Communications, Driving, Gunnery, Interest, Navigation, Piloting, Protocol, Security Systems, Sensor Operations, Streetwise, Support Weapons, Thrown Weapons, and Tracking require concrete subskills or affiliation/vehicle/GM-dependent subjects whose complete source-defined domains are not yet governed in Point Buy. MedTech and other Field-provided parameterized identities continue through their existing Life Module/Field paths. No unsupported open subject is converted into a fake generic purchase.

The source defines linked Attributes, specialties, Target Numbers, and Complexity Ratings in the same chapter. The catalog records source pages, while record-sheet linked-Attribute and TN/Complexity values remain explicit unavailable states where the current model lacks the required gameplay dependencies. Missing gameplay automation is advisory; unresolved concrete identity or legality remains a readiness blocker.

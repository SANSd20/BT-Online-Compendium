# Clan Warrior Field Skill Allocations

Alpha Slice 138 models the source-backed additional award used by Clan Warrior
Washout and the same governed shape used by later Clan life-module work.

The corrected third printing grants **+25 XP each to any three concrete Skills
in the character's Clan Warrior Fields** (p. 85). The creator therefore requires
exactly three distinct concrete Skill destinations and applies exactly 25 XP to
each; the award cannot be split or redirected to Attributes, Traits, unacquired
Fields, or reference-only Fields.

The current public Alpha exposes only acquired mechanical Clan Warrior Fields:
Clan Aerospace Warrior, Clan Elemental, and Clan MechWarrior. Clan Cavalry and
Clan ProtoMech Warrior remain source-backed reference data until their mechanics
are implemented. Clan Basic Training is not a Clan Warrior Field for this
allocation.

Selections retain the award and module identity, Field identity, concrete Skill,
XP amount, source citation, and provenance. Resolution is transactional: all
three selections are validated before any XP or ledger record is committed.
The ledger is part of the character save and portable export/import model.

# Stage 4 Real Life Module Audit — Alpha Slice 115

Authoritative source: *A Time of War — Corrected Third Printing*, Stage 4 pp. 84–91, with applicable v4.0 Errata. The source was retrieved through authenticated Box access because direct local Box Drive access is denied.

## Inventory

The source-defined Stage 4 catalog contains: Agitator (p. 84), Civilian Job (p. 84), Clan Watch Operative (p. 84), Clan Warrior Washout (p. 85), Cloister Training (p. 85), Combat Correspondent (p. 85), ComStar/Word of Blake (p. 85), Covert Operations (p. 86), Dark Caste (p. 87), Explorer (p. 87), Goliath Scorpion Seeker (p. 88), Guerilla Insurgent (p. 88), Merchant (p. 88), Ne’er-do-well (p. 89), Organized Crime (p. 89), Postgraduate Studies (p. 89), ProtoMech Pilot Training (p. 89), Scientist Caste Service (p. 90), Solaris Insider (p. 90), Solaris VII Games (p. 90), Think Tank (p. 90), Tour of Duty (p. 90), To Serve and Protect (p. 91), and Travel (p. 91).

For every source module the audit recorded module cost, time, prerequisites, fixed awards, flexible awards, conditional branches, and repetition notes. Existing implementation remains bounded to modules whose award and prerequisite choices are represented by the current public-alpha model.

## Slice 115 result

Travel is now supported at `stage4.travel`: 700 XP, +6 years, no TDS, Extra Income or Wealth +2 TP prerequisite, source-backed fixed awards and skill choices, 110 flexible XP, and repeatable Stage 4 behavior using the existing repeat policy.

The remaining source modules are intentionally deferred. Their blockers include caste/warrior status, affiliation subtypes, conditional Military or Intelligence/Police field allocations, branch-specific choices, skill-field acquisition, and source choices not expressible by the current UI/model. No unsupported awards were approximated.

## Verification boundary

Catalog, engine, UI preview/selection, persistence model compatibility, and automated checks cover the new Travel path. Full release verification is recorded in the Slice 115 completion report and deployment metadata.

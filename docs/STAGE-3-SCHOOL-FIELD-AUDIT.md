# Stage 3 school and Field audit - Alpha Slice 114

Source authority: *A Time of War — Corrected Third Printing*, printed pp. 82–85 and the Master Skill Fields List pp. 92–95, with applicable v4.0 Errata. Stage 3 requires one Basic Field, optionally one or more Advanced Fields, and permits a Special Field only after an Advanced Field; no school may select more than three Fields. Field costs, chronology, prerequisites, and same-transaction dependency ordering remain governed by the existing Stage 3 engine.

## Complete source school inventory

The source master list defines ten ordinary Stage 3 school types: Technical College, Trade School, University, Solaris Internship, Police Academy, Intelligence Operative Training, Military Academy, Military Enlistment, Family Training, and Officer Training School. The current catalog contains all ten stable school identities. Officer Training School is represented as the source-defined special addition with its single Officer Field selection.

All ten schools preserve source-page provenance, XP costs, automatic awards, flexible XP, school prerequisites, Basic/Advanced/Special Field offers, chronology, and the existing dropdown-first preview → choice resolution → Continue → commit workflow. No additional school identity was necessary in Slice 114.

## Field inventory and coverage

The current catalog retains 56 reference Fields and 50 mechanical Fields. The mechanical catalog covers the source civilian, intelligence/police, military, and special Field identities used by the supported schools, including Basic Training, Communications, Technician, Pilot, Cartographer, Engineer, Medical, Intelligence/Police, Military, and Special selections. Field definitions preserve canonical IDs, Basic/Advanced/Special classification, source pages, prerequisites, component Skills, variable subskill domains, affiliation-bound choices, and chronology.

The six reference-only Fields are Clan-dependent and remain unavailable because Clan creation, caste/phenotype branch selection, and the mandatory Clan-specific dependencies are outside this slice. No reference-only Field was promoted with approximated prerequisites or awards. Equipment remains 84 records and IDs.

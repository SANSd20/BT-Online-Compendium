# Clan Stage 4 Careers — Alpha Slice 139

Slice 139 records source-backed Stage 4 career definitions from *A Time of War*, Corrected Third Printing, pp. 84–90. Clan Watch Operative costs 1,200 XP and takes 3 years; Civilian Job costs 600 XP and takes 6 years; Scientist Caste Service costs 1,200 XP and takes 4 years.

Clan Watch Operative has governed Homeworld Clan and Invader Clan branch packages. Civilian Job uses atomic governed allocation of four distinct concrete career-Field Skills at 20 XP each. Scientist Caste Service retains its atomic Trait-pair package. Natural Aptitude remains deferred until a supported concrete Interest or Science destination exists.

All three modules remain internal source-backed definitions. Clan creation, Clan Stage 4 player controls, and Clan readiness remain disabled. Repetition metadata preserves full module cost, repeated Skills/flexible XP, and first-occurrence-only Attribute/Trait awards.

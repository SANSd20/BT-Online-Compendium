# Alpha Slice 151 — Deferred Life Module Prerequisites

Slice 151 aligns Life Module prerequisite timing with the Corrected Third Printing, p. 61: numeric Attribute, Skill, and Trait requirements may remain outstanding during module selection, but must be satisfied by final character design. Structural restrictions remain immediate.

The existing `prerequisiteIssues` ledger remains the canonical obligation record. Clan direct transactions now preserve the complete Trueborn Sibko MechWarrior awards while recording outstanding numeric requirements for later reevaluation instead of rejecting the transaction before award application. Missing identity, affiliation, caste, Phenotype, prior-history, duplicate-module, and concrete-choice requirements remain immediate failures.

Attribute minimum evaluation now uses effective Attribute levels from the shared legality model, including Phenotype modifiers, while retaining purchased XP, attained base level, Phenotype maximum, and Exceptional Attribute as separate concepts. Phenotype modifiers do not grant XP.

Final Review continues to reevaluate the canonical character state after allocations and Optimization. Outstanding prerequisites remain readiness blockers; resolved obligations are refreshed and do not remain stale. Clan identity and other unsupported Clan foundations remain explicit readiness blockers, so Slice 151 does not activate player-facing Clan creation or Clan readiness.

XP, chronology, award provenance, persistence, and existing Inner Sphere behavior remain unchanged. The MechWarrior Sibko pathway may now be represented internally with outstanding BOD/DEX/WIL obligations, but it is not ready for play until those requirements are legally satisfied during final design.

# Clan Affiliation Context — Alpha Slice 132

Slice 132 adds an internal, provenance-bearing affiliation context for the two
source-defined Clan categories: Invading Clan and Homeworld Clan. Both use the
canonical affiliation identity `affiliation.clan`, English as the primary
language, and the source-defined concrete Protocol subject `Clan`.

The contexts are intentionally separate from the player-facing supported
affiliation list. They can be used by governed domain fixtures and future Clan
packages without enabling Clan selection, Clan XP awards, Phenotypes, Fields,
or Life Modules in the creator.

Source: *A Time of War — Corrected Third Printing*, printed pp. 69–70;
applicable v4.0 errata reviewed. The source assigns Protocol/Clan as a
concrete Skill destination in both affiliation packages. No unrestricted
`Protocol/Clan` free-text identity is created; the existing affiliation-bound
Skill resolver supplies the canonical `skill.protocol` destination with the
`Clan` subject.

Clan caste, Phenotype, Field Aptitude, Clan Fields, Clan Life Modules, and
readiness remain unsupported and continue to block Clan characters from play.

# Clan Skill Fields — Alpha Slice 134

The Master Skill Fields list on printed page 95 of *A Time of War — Corrected
Third Printing* identifies six Clan Fields. Slice 134 promotes only the Fields
whose source prerequisites and component bundles are fully expressible by the
existing governed catalog:

| Field | Source cost | Mechanical status |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Clan Basic Training | 144 XP | Supported catalog entry |
| Clan Aerospace Warrior | 200 XP | Supported catalog entry |
| Clan Elemental | 280 XP | Supported catalog entry |
| Clan MechWarrior | 240 XP | Supported catalog entry |
| Clan Cavalry | 200 XP | Reference-only |
| Clan ProtoMech Warrior | 200 XP | Reference-only |

The source costs are field acquisition costs calculated from the source’s
component count and the applicable per-skill cost; they are not granted by
catalog metadata. Existing school-offer governance remains authoritative, and
Slice 134 does not invent Clan schools or activate player-facing Clan
creation, Clan Field purchasing, or Clan Life Modules.

The four supported entries preserve the source-defined Clan affiliation,
Phenotype, Attribute minimums, and component Skills. Phenotype prerequisites
are now evaluated by both character validation and life-module prerequisite
resolution. Clan Field Aptitude remains a separate, non-purchasable −1 TN
effect for eligible trueborn characters.

Clan Cavalry remains reference-only because its source bundle requires a
governed alternative between Driving/Any and Piloting/Air Vehicle, plus
vehicle-environment alternatives for Gunnery and Tactics. The current
component model cannot express those alternatives exactly, so no incomplete
or fictitious mechanical choice was added. Clan ProtoMech Warrior remains
reference-only because its mandatory EI Neural Implant and associated chemical
addiction support are not yet implemented.

Source authority: *A Time of War — Corrected Third Printing*, p. 95 and the
previously audited Phenotype/Field Aptitude material on pp. 121–122; applicable
*A Time of War v4.0 Errata* reviewed.

# Clan Phenotype and Attribute Legality — Alpha Slice 120

The existing Core Phenotype catalog preserves the corrected-printing Aerospace, Elemental, and MechWarrior Attribute modifiers, maxima, and source bonus-Trait metadata. Slice 120 makes the eligibility boundary explicit: a Clan-only Phenotype requires the schema-only Clan identity, while ordinary characters remain on Normal Human. This does not activate Clan creation, apply phenotype awards, or remove the existing final-review blocker for unsupported Clan state.

Attribute legality continues to keep purchased/base Attribute score, free Phenotype modifier, Phenotype maximum, and Exceptional Attribute adjustment separate. Exceptional Attribute remains a target-specific +2 TP Trait and can raise the modeled maximum by one when fully active; it does not authorize Clan identity, Phenotype selection, or unsupported package awards.

Source: *A Time of War — Corrected Third Printing*, pp. 95–97, 116, and 121–122; applicable v4.0 errata reviewed. Clan-only Phenotype selection remains unavailable in the player-facing workflow until Clan Fields, Skills/Traits, Life Modules, and chronology are supported.

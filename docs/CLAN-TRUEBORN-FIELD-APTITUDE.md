# Clan Trueborn and Field Aptitude Foundation — Alpha Slice 124

Slice 124 adds provenance-bearing, unsupported metadata for the source-defined Clan birth-origin distinction and Phenotype-linked Field Aptitude. `trueborn` and `freeborn` are stable identity values; they do not automatically select a Phenotype, grant XP, create a Bloodname, or unlock Clan training. Field Aptitude records preserve the Phenotype and associated Field identities without applying a generic Trait bonus or Field XP.

The Corrected Third Printing defines Trueborn on p. 109, distinguishes Freeborn natural birth in the Clan glossary, and references Field Aptitude with Phenotype examples on p. 121. Trueborn is relevant to Bloodname eligibility; Freeborn characters do not require a Phenotype Trait. Clan affiliation, caste, Sibko/training, source Field prerequisites, chronology, and Bloodright/Bloodname mechanics remain unsupported. Final Review continues to block any character containing Clan foundation metadata.
